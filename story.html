A journey written in love

OUR STORY

From strangers, to best friends, to forever.

Sometimes, the most beautiful love stories begin in the most unexpected ways.

For two years, we kept crossing paths at the production area. We would see each other from time to time, but we were nothing more than strangers. Little did we know that those ordinary encounters were quietly leading us to a beautiful story we would someday call our own.

One day, I went to his station to talk to a friend. While we were having a conversation, Steven (Hu Sheng Shih) came closer, and I asked him a simple question: "Are you okay?" He simply said, "No."

That simple answer made me curious. I wondered what was going on with him and why he wasn't okay. Out of curiosity, I asked our mutual friend for his social media account. And just like that, a simple question became the beginning of countless conversations.

We started as strangers, slowly became friends, and eventually became best friends. Through our everyday conversations, shared moments, laughter, and time spent together, I found myself slowly falling for him.

One day, I asked him, "Don't you ever see yourself having someone by your side?" His answer was a simple "No." But I gathered the courage to tell him that I was starting to like him. At that time, he wasn't quite sure if he was falling for me too.

Still, I never expected that the little feelings growing inside me would eventually lead us somewhere beautiful.

Then came a moment when someone else was being introduced to me. I talked to Steven and told him that maybe we wouldn't get to spend as much time together anymore because I was about to go on a date with someone else.

Perhaps the thought of losing what we had made him realize something. He asked me, "Why don't we give us a try?" And that was when our love story truly began.

What started with a simple question, an unexpected conversation, and a growing friendship slowly turned into a love we never imagined we would have.

From two strangers who used to cross paths at work, to friends, to best friends, and eventually to lovers — we have now spent four beautiful years choosing each other, growing together, and creating memories along the way.

Looking back, it amazes us how life works. For two years, we were just strangers passing by, never knowing that one day, we would become each other's home.

We didn't meet through a grand romantic gesture. We met through an ordinary moment that became extraordinary.

And now, after four years of love, friendship, patience, and countless memories, we are ready to begin another chapter — not just as boyfriend and girlfriend, but as husband and wife.

Who would have thought that one simple question, "Are you okay?" would lead us to a lifetime of "I do"?

Our story began unexpectedly, but our forever will be a choice we make every single day.

From strangers to best friends, from best friends to lovers, and now, from lovers to forever.

With love,
Princess & Steven (Hu Sheng Shih) 💚
